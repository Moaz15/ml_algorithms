DECISION TREES - Senior MLE Interview Notes

How to read this file:
  Section 1 is the full subtopic checklist - the roadmap for this topic.
  Each group is expanded in its own file (group 1.1 -> foundations.txt),
  written the way I would say it out loud in an interview: plain words, a real-world
  example, and the follow-up an interviewer asks next.
  A tag like [2.1] on a checklist line means that subtopic is written up as section 2.1
  of that group's file.
  A blank tag [   ] means it is still to be written.
  Scope: a single tree only. Bagging, Random Forest and boosting live in Ensemble_Learning.
